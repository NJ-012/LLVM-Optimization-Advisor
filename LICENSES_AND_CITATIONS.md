# Third-party datasets and references

This starter contains original small benchmark kernels written for the project. It does not bundle PolyBench/C source.

For standard benchmark expansion, PolyBench/C 4.2.1 is a suite of 30 numerical kernels. Upstream repository: https://github.com/MatthiasJReisinger/PolyBenchC-4.2.1 . Review and preserve the upstream `LICENSE.txt`, AUTHORS, and notices if you download or redistribute it.

CompilerGym documents LLVM optimization environments and datasets such as CBench, BLAS, and AnghaBench: https://compilergym.com/llvm/index.html . CompilerGym is optional and not required by this MVP.
